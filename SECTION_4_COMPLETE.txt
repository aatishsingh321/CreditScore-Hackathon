FAIRNESS METRICS DETAILS
================================================================================

Statistical Parity Difference (SPD):
- Measures: Difference in positive outcome rates
- Formula: P(positive | group=A) - P(positive | group=B)
- Threshold: <0.1 for fairness
- Results: Gender (0.0195), Age (0.0146), Marital (0.0315)

Disparate Impact Ratio (DIR):
- Measures: Ratio of positive outcomes
- Formula: P(positive | unprivileged) / P(positive | privileged)
- Threshold: >0.8 (80% rule)
- Results: Gender (0.8583), Age (0.8958), Marital (0.7698)

80% Rule (EEOC Guideline):
- Standard: DIR must be ≥0.8
- Interpretation: Unprivileged group gets ≥80% of privileged rate
- Results: Gender ✅, Age ✅, Marital ❌
